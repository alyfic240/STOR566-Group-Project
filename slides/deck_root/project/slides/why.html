MOTIVATION

Clean test accuracy is not what a deployed classifier sees

Cheap cameras add noise. A face cropped from a wide shot is a few dozen pixels across.

FER-2013 images are already 48 × 48 grayscale, and their labels are noisy.

The labels describe facial expressions. They do not establish what a person feels.

RQ1

How well does each method classify the original images?

RQ2

How does each method perform as noise increases or resolution decreases, and how much of its original accuracy does it retain?

RQ3

Does noise augmentation help on noisy images and transfer to reduced resolution? How does it affect accuracy on the original images?
