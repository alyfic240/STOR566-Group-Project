TAKEAWAYS

Three things to remember

1

Clean accuracy ranks models correctly only on clean images.

53 → 61 → 69% on original images; all three at the majority-class rate by σ = 0.2.

2

Pre-training bought accuracy, not robustness.

ResNet-18 against the small CNN: +8.1 points on original images, +0.3 under noise.

3

Noise augmentation is cheap insurance, most of all for pretrained models.

+10.0 to +18.9 points under noise; +9.2 (ResNet-18) and +3.9 (ViT-B/16) at low resolution, which it never saw.
