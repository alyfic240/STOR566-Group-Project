LIMITATIONS

What these results do not show

One dataset

48 × 48 grayscale faces with noisy labels. The labels are expressions, not emotions.

Two synthetic degradations

Applied one at a time. Real images mix blur, noise, compression and pose.

Confounded models

Architecture, size and pre-training change together, so no single cause can be named.

Three seeds

The standard deviations are rough. The main effects are many of them wide.

Tuning budget

Three learning rates per model; the chosen one is at the grid edge for both pretrained models. One step beyond changed the score by less than its seed SD.

No other augmentation

No flips or crops, by design, so clean accuracy is below the best published numbers.

0:45. One dataset with noisy labels. Two synthetic degradations, one at a time. The three methods differ in architecture, size and pre-training at once, so we compare methods, not causes. And no flips or crops, so that noise is the only augmentation; that keeps the comparison clean and costs some clean accuracy.
